The porch

When the porch is lit, Porchlight answers people who knock in #porchlight (they send porchlight) with the channels heard here, and announces channels that are new here. It speaks only in #porchlight, as "Porchlight", through its own companion on this repeater.

    Channels and keys

    Messages are decoded from the repeater's packet log. Changes apply within a few seconds.

    Name sources

    Lists of hashtag channel names, fetched daily and tried against channels without a key. Add your local CoreScope (its address), a channel catalog (a .json link) or a plain list of names (.txt or .txt.gz).
